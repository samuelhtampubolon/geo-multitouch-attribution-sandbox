Pillar 3 · ROAS & Budget

Budget optimizer with diminishing returns

Each channel's revenue curve rk(x) = ak·xβ is calibrated to its Markov-attributed revenue at today's spend. The optimum equalizes marginal ROAS across channels (KKT), inside a trust region so no channel swings more than δ.

Scenario

Lower β saturates faster, so spreading spend matters more.

        Reference

        Governing equations

        Every formula below is implemented in src/services/ (Python) and web/engine.js, and checked by the test suite.

        Share of Model

        SoM=1M∑i=1Cwi·1log2(ranki+1)·(1+0.5si)

        M prompt evaluations, wi = 1 if cited, si ∈ [−1, 1]. Bounded by 1.5, so SoMnorm = SoM / 1.5 ∈ [0, 1].

        Share of Voice & concentration

        SoVb=SoMb∑jSoMj,HHI=10000∑bSoVb2

        HHI below 1,500 is unconcentrated, above 2,500 highly concentrated. Engine spread uses Shannon entropy H = −∑ p log₂ p.

        Wilson score interval

        p^+z22n±zp^(1−p^)n+z24n21+z2/n

        95% interval (z = 1.96) for the citation rate. Unlike the Wald interval it never leaves [0, 1].

        Absorbing Markov chain

        P=(QR0I),N=(I−Q)−1,B=NR

        P(conversion) = Bstart, conv, solved as (I − Q)b = r by LU / Gaussian elimination, not by explicit inversion.

        Removal effect

        Rk=1−P(conv∣chain without k)P(conv∣full chain),Wk=Rk∑jRj

        Order-k chains use states (ct−k+1, …, ct); removing k sends every state containing k to NULL.

        Flow conservation (verified)

        Nstart,c=# touches of c# journeys,Bstart,conv=# conversions# journeys

        For the maximum-likelihood chain, expected visits and conversion probability reproduce the observed data exactly, for every order k.

        Exact Shapley value

        φk=∑S⊆N∖{k}|S|!(|N|−|S|−1)!|N|![v(S∪{k})−v(S)]

        v(S) = value of converted journeys whose channels ⊆ S. Efficiency: ∑ φk = v(N).

        Shapley closed form

        v=∑Tvalue(T)uT⇒φk=∑T∋kvalue(T)|T|

        The game is a sum of unanimity games, so the exact value costs O(journeys). Used above 12 channels; tested equal to 2N enumeration.

        Position-based & time-decay

        wi={0.4i∈{first,last}0.2L−2otherwisewi∝2−(L−1−i)/h

        U-shaped 40/20/40 (50/50 for two touches); time decay halves credit every h touches before conversion.

        Model divergence

        JSD=12KL(P∥M)+12KL(Q∥M),TV=12∑k|pk−qk|

        M = (P + Q)/2. JSD is symmetric and bounded by 1 bit; TV is the fraction of credit that moves between models.

        Concave budget optimum (KKT)

        maxx∑kakxkβs.t.∑kxk=B,xk*=[(βakλ)11−β]LkUk

        ak = revenuek / spendkβ; λ* (equilibrium marginal ROAS) found by bisection on ∑ xk(λ) = B.

        Bootstrap percentile CI

        n(b)∼Multinomial(n,π^),CI95=[Wk(2.5%),Wk(97.5%)]

        Journeys are resampled with replacement, the chain is refit, and Wk is recomputed for every replicate.
